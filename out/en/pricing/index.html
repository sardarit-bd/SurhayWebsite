What moves the price

Five factors decide whether a project lands at the lower or upper end of the range.

Number of pages
Not every page costs the same: five pages on one pattern are cheaper than three that all look different.
State of your content
If copy and images exist, things move fast. If they still have to be created, that is the biggest time factor in the project.
Integrations
CRM, booking system, inventory or newsletter: every integration is a small project of its own.
Bilingual setup
A second language does not double the work, but it does increase it noticeably — especially in maintenance.
Deadline pressure
A launch in three instead of seven weeks is possible and carries a surcharge. We tell you how much beforehand.
