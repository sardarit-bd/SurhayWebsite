03 · Services

SEO & Performance

A website nobody finds is a business card in a drawer. We build the technical groundwork so search engines understand your pages, and we measure load time and Core Web Vitals instead of talking about them.

Pricing
base setup included · ongoing from €190/month
Process
Setup within the project · effect after 3–6 months

Included

  • Technical foundation

    Sitemap, robots.txt, clean URLs, canonicals, hreflang, 301 redirects and indexable legal pages. The basics most sites fail on.

  • Core Web Vitals

    LCP, CLS and INP are measured and optimised — not estimated. Target: green on mobile, not only in a desktop test.

  • Structured data

    Markup for organisation, services, articles, FAQ and breadcrumbs in schema.org format. So Google understands your page rather than merely reading it.

  • Keyword and page structure

    Which page answers which search intent is decided before writing. One page per intent — never two competing with each other.

  • Local visibility

    Setting up and maintaining the Google Business Profile, consistent company data and local landing pages when your customers come from the region.

  • Reporting you can actually read

    Search Console and privacy-friendly analytics without a cookie banner. On request, a monthly two-paragraph report instead of a 40-page PDF.

Process

From first call to launch — in four steps.

  1. 01

    Baseline

    Where do you stand today? Indexing, rankings, load times, technical errors — as a prioritised list, not a tool export.

  2. 02

    Technology first

    We fix what actively blocks search engines first. Optimising content on a technically broken site is wasted work.

  3. 03

    Content by intent

    Every page gets one clear search intent, a matching heading structure and copy that actually answers the question.

  4. 04

    Re-measure

    After four, twelve and twenty-four weeks. What does not work gets changed — not massaged into a nicer chart.

Target values

What you can hold us to

These values are fixed before the project starts and measured again after launch. You get the measurement itself, not a summary of one.

  • 2.5 seconds

    Time to main content

    The largest visible area of your page is in place within 2.5 seconds at the latest. That is the threshold at which Google rates a page as fast — measured on mobile data, not on office Wi-Fi.

  • 200 milliseconds

    Response to a click

    That is the most it may take for the page to answer an input visibly. Above it a website feels sluggish, even when it loaded quickly.

  • 90 points and up

    Lighthouse, measured on mobile

    Across all four categories: performance, accessibility, best practices and SEO. You get the full report before sign-off, unabridged.

  • 100 per cent of pages

    Technically complete

    No subpage without its own title, its own description, a clean heading order and an entry in the sitemap. The whole site is checked, not the homepage.

  • 3 directories

    Findable locally

    Google Business Profile, Apple Business Connect and Bing Places — the same name, the same address, the same phone number everywhere. Inconsistencies are the most common reason local businesses go unfound.

  • 4 weeks

    First review

    Four weeks after launch we look at the numbers together: which pages bring visitors, which lead to enquiries, and where the path breaks off.
